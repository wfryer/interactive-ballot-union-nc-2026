How to use this page

  1. Compare it with your own official sample ballot from the N.C. State Board of Elections Voter Search. Your races depend on your address, so yours may differ.
  2. Follow the links under each name to learn about that person. Campaign websites show how a candidate presents themselves. Third-party pages are listed when no campaign site was found.
  3. Tap My pick to track your choices. They are saved only in this browser. Then print your cheat sheet at the bottom of the page.

Why this project exists

Interactive Ballot is part of Heal Our Culture, an effort to reduce political polarization and help neighbors engage as informed citizens. When voters learn about every candidate on their ballot, not only the names in the headlines, our conversations get less angry and our choices get better.

The goal is twofold. First, to encourage voters to arrive at the polls better informed. Second, to encourage candidates to use their websites and other digital resources to explain their ideas, experience, background, and fitness for office, so that voters can find those answers easily. When a candidate has no website, this page says so plainly.

This project is nonpartisan, experimental, and not affiliated with any candidate, campaign, political party, or election office. Links are provided for convenience and civic learning. Candidates, districts, and websites can change, so always verify with the N.C. State Board of Elections and the Union County Board of Elections (704-283-3809).
